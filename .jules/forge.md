**[Replacing Pass-By-Value with References in Iterator Pipelines]**
**Learning:** Automatically fixing `needless_pass_by_value` across mapping functions often introduces `E0308` type mismatches down the line, specifically requiring `into_iter().map(...)` to be swapped for `iter().map(...)`. Additionally, `Option` mapping often requires `.as_ref()` before mapping to borrow.
**Action:** When manually refactoring `needless_pass_by_value` for structs like `Row` objects extracted from the database, carefully trace the `collect()` and map chains immediately afterward to substitute `iter()` and `.as_ref()` properly.
