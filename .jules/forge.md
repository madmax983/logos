**Refactoring `logos-store-pg`'s God Functions**
**Learning:** `crates/logos-store-pg/src/store.rs` contained massive god functions `write_import_batch` and `write_reconciliation_run_and_month_close`. They performed complex setup and transactional multi-table inserts. This triggered clippy warnings (`too_many_lines`) and hampered readability.
**Action:** Extract the transactional row insertion payloads into discrete, private helper functions `insert_import_batch` and `insert_reconciliation_run_and_month_close` so the root functions act purely as guards and coordinators. Make sure to implement them on `PostgresStore`, not the `LedgerStore` trait!
